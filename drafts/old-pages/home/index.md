# Coordinate frames

A coordinate frame is an agreed way of saying **where something is**.

To understand any frame, ask four questions:

1. **Where is zero?**
2. **Which way do the axes point?**
3. **What is it attached to?** The spinning Earth, the stars, a spot on the ground, or a vehicle?
4. **What are the numbers?** Distances, or angles?

Every frame page starts with an ID card that answers them.
