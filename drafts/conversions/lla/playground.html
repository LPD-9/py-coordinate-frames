The equations for the globe

Name: spherical to Cartesian conversion

The job: turn two angles and a height into three straight-line distances from the Earth's centre.

How: start with R + h, the pin's full distance from the centre. Then use sin and cos to work out how much of it points in each direction.

R is the Earth's radius (6,371 km) and h is altitude in km. A minus answer means the opposite direction. Finally, the page turns the globe to match the side you're looking from.

The equations for the flat map

Name: equirectangular projection (or plate carrée)

The job: turn two angles into a spot on a flat rectangle. Altitude isn't used.

How: shift each angle so it starts at zero, then divide by its full range. That tells you how much of the way along the map you are.
