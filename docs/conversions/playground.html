LLA → ECEF

The job: swap two angles and a height for three straight distances.

a = 6,378.137 km is the distance from the centre to the equator. e² = 0.00669438 says how squashed the Earth is. RN is usually written N, but here N means north.

ECEF → LLA

The job: swap three straight distances for two angles and a height.

ECEF → NED

The job: move zero to your spot, then turn the axes to face north, east and down.

The little 0 means "of zero". So φ₀ and λ₀ are zero's latitude and longitude, and X₀, Y₀, Z₀ are zero's ECEF.

NED → ECEF

The job: the same thing backwards. Turn the axes back, then move zero back to the Earth's centre.

The answers use unrounded numbers, so a calculator may differ slightly in the last digit.
